Legal AI Governance

For Managing Partners, COLPs and Legal IT Directors

An approved AI policy is not the same as safe AI practice.

Many medium sized and large UK law firms now have an AI policy approved at board level. Fewer can demonstrate with confidence how AI is used in day to day practice: which tools are being used, what client data is entered and what checks take place before work leaves the firm. Our From Policy to Practice engagement brings policy, controls and day to day practice into line over about six weeks, in three fixed fee phases.

The EIS Augmentation Method

Whether we are working with a law firm or a government department, every engagement follows four principles designed to deliver visible outcomes, strengthen internal capability and support an orderly transfer of ownership.

1

Senior capability, proportionate commitment

You gain experienced technology leadership for the period and level of commitment the work requires, without the cost or delay associated with a permanent senior appointment.

2

Specialist work in specialist hands

Each task is led by someone with relevant experience in a comparable environment, reducing the time and risk involved in building knowledge during delivery.

3

Embedded delivery, visible change

We work inside your teams and processes. Success is measured by what changes in operation, not by the volume of reports produced.

4

Engagements designed for effective transition

Every engagement includes a defined exit and a deliberate transfer of knowledge, responsibility and capability to your team. Success means that your people can take full ownership of the next phase.
