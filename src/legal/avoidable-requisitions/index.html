Assistance with avoidable requisitions.

Each requisition raised by HM Land Registry takes fee earner time, delays registration and may affect client confidence. Many can be avoided. We help firms identify where they originate and address them at source.

A cost that rarely appears in management reporting

Requisitions are usually handled individually, by the fee earner responsible for the file. Each one may appear minor, but the pattern can remain unseen: the same omissions and errors recurring across teams, templates and systems. Over time, the cost accumulates in rework, in delayed registrations and in the risk of an application being cancelled if a reply is missed.

The Defensible Requisition Blueprint

1

Diagnose

We establish your firm's rate of avoidable requisitions and where the causes lie, whether in process, templates, case management configuration or training.

2

Address the causes

Practical changes to checklists, precedents and system workflows, developed with your conveyancing team.

3

Measure

A simple, continuing view of requisition rates, so that improvement can be seen and sustained.

4

Transfer ownership

Your team takes responsibility for the controls and the measurement at the end of the engagement.
