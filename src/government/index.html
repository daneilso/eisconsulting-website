Where programmes stall

In our experience, the constraint is more often a shortage of experience in particular places than a shortage of people.

Decisions without owners

Decisions that should take days can wait for weeks when nobody with the right authority and context is close enough to the work.

Dependencies outside the team's control

Delivery teams may be ready to proceed but held back by another team, a supplier or a legacy system they cannot influence directly.

Plans that are slow to become services

Business cases and roadmaps are approved, but working services can arrive later than intended.

A different delivery model

Large systems integrators are well suited to many programmes. Where the need is for experienced judgement rather than additional capacity, a smaller and more senior team may be more effective.

What we provide
